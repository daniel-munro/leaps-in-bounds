# Stamp folding

## Status

- n = 1 to 13: lower bounds certified by listing every folding (exact counts). The lists are generated by depth-first search, building the stack from the bottom and matching each edge's creases like brackets, and match OEIS A000136.
- n = 1 to 3: upper bounds proved in Lean from the trivial bound n! (`StampFoldingSmall.lean`).
- n = 14 and up: a full list is too large for the 500 kB limit (n = 13 is 288 kB with xz, and the counts grow by about 3.5 per stamp). Only partial lists, giving weaker lower bounds, fit.

## Ideas

- Upper bounds for n ≥ 4 need a Lean proof that counts the foldings, such as a verified enumeration by the same bracket-matching search, proved equivalent to the definition in `Folding.lean`. Even n = 4 by `decide` over all 24 permutations may be slow in the kernel.
